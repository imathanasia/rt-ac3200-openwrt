Flashing / reverting: use ASUS recovery mode (hold Reset while powering on until the Power LED blinks slowly; PC at 192.168.1.10) with the ASUS Firmware Restoration utility or curl -T firmware.trx tftp://192.168.1.1/. To go back to ASUS firmware, flash RT-AC3200_384.13_10.trx the same way. Never 30-30-30 on this board.
